# Wavepod: video brief

Give this file and the screenshots in [`screenshots/`](screenshots/) to the video creator, or paste it into an AI video tool together
with the images.

---

## Prompt

Create a **30-second product video** for **Wavepod**, a free Mac app that turns any podcast into a transcript you can
read, a summary you can click, and an episode you can ask questions. Everything runs on the Mac.

**Formats:** 16:9 (1920×1080) for YouTube and X, and a 9:16 (1080×1920) cut for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. In 9:16,
crop into the part of each screenshot that matters, don't shrink the whole window.

**Style:** calm, premium, Apple-like. Warm paper background (#F4F1EA in light mode), deep teal accent (#1F4E4F),
serif headlines. Slow, smooth camera moves on the screenshots: gentle push-ins and pans, ease-in-out, no spins,
no glitch effects, no bouncing. Cross-fade or slide between shots (about 0.3 s). Every motion ends still for a
beat so the text can be read.

**Music:** soft, modern, low-key electronic or acoustic, about 90–100 BPM. No vocals. Optional subtle UI clicks.

**Text on screen:** short, large, one line at a time, in the serif style. Never more than 7 words on screen.

**Voice-over (optional):** calm, warm voice. Script below.

### Don't

- Don't show a Windows or iPhone frame. It's a Mac app: use a MacBook frame or no frame.
- Don't add fake ratings, user counts or press logos.
- Don't speed-ramp or shake the camera.
