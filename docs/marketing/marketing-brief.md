## Prompt

You are writing marketing for **Wavepod**, a free, open-source Mac app. Use only the facts below. Do not invent
numbers, customers, reviews or features. Keep the tone calm, clear and confident, like Apple or Linear: short
sentences, concrete examples, no hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless", "AI-powered magic").

### One line

**Read any podcast, ask it anything. Open-source on your Mac, completely free**

### The problem

Podcasts are full of things worth keeping: an idea, a number, the exact way a guest put it. But audio is hard to
search, and you can't skim it. Finding "that thing they said about pricing" means scrubbing through hours.

### What Wavepod does

Paste a Spotify, Apple Podcasts or YouTube link. Wavepod downloads the episode and, on your Mac:

1. **Transcribes it**, with every speaker told apart and named.
2. **Writes a summary** organized under headings. Click any point to hear the moment it comes from.
3. **Answers your questions.** "What did they say about pricing?" The answer quotes the transcript, in the language
   you asked in. Click a quote to hear it.
4. **Follows your podcasts.** New episodes can be transcribed and summarized automatically.

### Key numbers (KPIs)

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Speed | A 4-hour episode is transcribed in less than 10 minutes on an M3 Mac |
| Privacy | 100 % of audio stays on your Mac. Transcription always runs locally |
| Cost | Free. Open source (Apache-2.0). No account, no subscription |
| Sources | Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, RSS feeds, podcast websites, local audio and video files |
| Languages | Most European languages, including English, Spanish and Catalan |
| Answers | On your Mac by default, or on your own ChatGPT or Claude plan if you choose |
| Setup | One-time model download: transcription plus an optional 2.3 GB Ask model |
| Requirements | Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 15 or later, about 4 GB free disk |
| Transparency | Settings › Data and Privacy lists every network request the app makes |

### Features, in order of importance

- **Paste any link.** Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, an RSS feed or a podcast's website. Or drop in an audio or
  video file.
- **Know who said what.** Wavepod tells the voices apart and names them from the episode itself. Name a voice once
  and it recognises that voice in the next episode of the same podcast.
- **Summary first.** The summary is ready when the transcript is. Each point links to its moment in the audio.
- **Ask the episode.** Answers come with quotes you can click to play.
- **Read and listen together.** The transcript follows the audio. Click any line to play from there.
- **Follow podcasts.** Turn on Auto-transcribe and new episodes arrive ready to read.
- **Private by design.** Audio never leaves your Mac. Questions go to ChatGPT or Claude only if you pick that.
- **Made for the Mac.** Native app, light and dark mode, keyboard shortcuts (⌘K to paste a link, ⌘J to ask),
  media keys, trackpad swipe to go back, adjustable reading font and size.

### Who it's for

- People who learn from podcasts: founders, students, researchers, journalists.
- Anyone who wants to quote a podcast accurately or find one moment in a long episode.
- People who care about privacy and don't want to upload audio to a cloud service.

### Proof points to show, not tell

- A 3-hour interview turned into a summary with headings and clickable points.
- A question typed in Spanish about an English episode, answered in Spanish with quotes.
- The speaker names next to each paragraph of the transcript.
- The privacy panel listing every network request.

### Do not say

- That it works on Windows, Linux, iPhone or Intel Macs.
- That it can download Spotify-exclusive episodes (it can't: Spotify doesn't publish their audio).
- That it has users, ratings or press coverage, unless you add real ones.
- That it is notarized by Apple, until a notarized release is out.
